About

ARKRAJ BISWAS

Environmental researcher working across climate, landscapes, sustainability and geospatial analysis.

I study how land, water and climate fit together — how small farms read erratic weather, how soils recover, how commons ponds and urban tree cover buffer heat and water stress, and how satellite imagery and maps make those patterns legible.

My approach is field-first and evidence-led: observe carefully, map honestly, write plainly, and show uncertainty rather than hiding it.
